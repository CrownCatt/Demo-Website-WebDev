What is BrightMind?

BrightMind is a fictional productivity product concept designed to help people organize priorities and make progress.

Do I need to be productive all day?

No. The concept is about choosing meaningful priorities and making space for rest, not filling every minute.

Is BrightMind available to use?

This page is a design demo, not a functioning productivity service. The dashboard interaction is a front-end demonstration.
